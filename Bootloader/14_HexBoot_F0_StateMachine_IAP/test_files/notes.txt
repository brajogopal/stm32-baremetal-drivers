Test Notes

Firmware File:

* 11_Hexboot_F0_TEST.bin

Packet File:

* 14
_Hexboot_F0_TEST_packet.bin

Packet Format:

* Header (1 byte)
* Payload Length (2 bytes)
* CRC16 (2 bytes)
* Payload (N bytes)

Test Configuration:

* STM32F030C8T6
* UART2
* Baud Rate: 9600
* Chunk Size: 128 bytes

Expected Results:

* Header detected successfully
* Payload length reconstructed correctly
* CRC reconstructed correctly
* Firmware received in 128-byte chunks
* Flash programmed successfully
* Calculated CRC matches received CRC
* Metadata updated successfully
* Bootloader validates and jumps to application

Known Limitations:

* Single receive buffer architecture
* UART interrupts disabled during flash programming
* Sender requires inter-chunk delay
* No ACK/NACK flow control
* No ping-pong buffering

Future Improvements:

* Ping-Pong (Double Buffer) Reception
* Continuous reception during flash programming
* ACK/NACK protocol
* DMA-based UART reception
